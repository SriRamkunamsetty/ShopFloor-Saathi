# Preventive Maintenance Schedule (demo manual)

Synthetic demo document. Intervals are examples, not manufacturer recommendations.

## Daily operator checks
Before the first shift: check coolant level, way-oil level, air pressure, and clear chips from the work area. Look and listen: unusual noise, smell or vibration must be reported to the supervisor before starting. Verify that the cooling fan is running and the cabinet vents are not covered.

## Weekly checks
Clean cabinet filters and radiator fins. Check coolant concentration with a refractometer. Inspect tool holders and clamp sets for wear. Check belt tension on the spindle drive.

## Monthly checks
Inspect the spindle drive terminals for heat discolouration, check the fuse ratings against the drawing, and test emergency stop and door interlocks. Review the tool life records and adjust replacement intervals.

## Energy saving practices
Switch off coolant pumps and conveyors during long idle periods. Avoid running the spindle at idle for more than a few minutes. Compressed air leaks waste energy: fix leaks found during weekly checks. Schedule heavy cycles outside peak tariff hours when the order allows.
